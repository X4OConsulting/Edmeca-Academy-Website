# EdMeCa Design System

> Business-Level Thinking. Entrepreneur-Ready Tools. Proven Results.

EdMeCa (Pty) Ltd is a South-African company that makes MBA-level business frameworks accessible to entrepreneurs through hands-on facilitation and AI-enabled tooling. Their tagline — **"From Framework to Execution to Evidence"** — sums up the offering: they take entrepreneurs from strategy through prototypes to investor-ready pitches.

This design system is the visual + tonal kit for building anything on the EdMeCa brand: marketing pages, one-pagers, programme decks, course collateral, partner pitches.

The **source of truth** is the official *EdMeca Logo & Brand Identity Guidelines, Jan 2024* (stored at `assets/EdMeca_Brand_Guide_2024.pdf`). Where the brand guide and the bizdev one-pager disagree, the brand guide wins.

## Content fundamentals

The voice on the one-pager is **professional, plain-spoken, and outcome- oriented**. It assumes a business-savvy reader (programme managers, deans, CSI heads) and treats them as peers, not as an audience to impress.

### Tone

- **Direct & confident**, not breathless. *"Single targeted intervention. Immediate impact with a clear, scoped outcome."*
- **Outcomes-led.** Copy keeps coming back to evidence, results, progression, impact reporting. The brand-line — *Business-Level Thinking. Entrepreneur-Ready Tools. Proven Results.* — is the template: rhythm of three, no fluff.
- **Educated but accessible.** They cite SWOT, PESTLE, BMC, MVP — but never hide behind jargon; every framework is paired with a verb (*"Pitch Prep & MVP Development"*).
- **Neutral pronouns.** Largely third-person and noun-led ("EdMeCa makes…", "Builds specific capabilities…"). When pronouns appear, "we" / "our" — never "I". The reader is never directly addressed as "you" on the one-pager.

### Casing

- **Section labels in ALL CAPS** with wide letter-spacing — *WHO WE ARE, WHO WE SERVE, OUR PROGRAMMES, THREE WAYS TO ENGAGE*. Treat these as eyebrow labels, not headings.
- **Title Case** for programme names — *AI Masterclass for Executives, Business Model Canvas, Pitch Prep & MVP Development*.
- **Sentence case** for descriptive copy.

### Mechanics

- **No emoji.** The brand reads serious B2B / public-sector; emoji would read juvenile. Use icons (line-style, two-tone) instead.
- **Em-dashes, not exclamation marks.** *"…AI-enabled tooling that turns strategy into action, prototypes into products, and ideas into investor- ready pitches."*
- **Lists are short and parallel** — three to five items, same grammatical shape across the set.
- **Numerals as digits** for tiers and dosage (4–5 interventions, 12-month partnership) — they read like a contract.
- **Ampersands are allowed** in section labels and pairings (*Clients & Partners*, *AI & Technology*) for the compact, programmatic feel.

### Phrases the brand reaches for

- "framework", "intervention", "tooling", "progression", "measurable", "outcome", "baseline-to-outcome", "investor-ready", "scoped".
- The triadic refrain — *Strategy → Action · Prototype → Product · Idea → Pitch.* Reuse this pattern when you need a rhythm.

## Visual foundations

### Palette — official Pantone family (brand guide §5)

| Token | Hex | Pantone / RGB | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--brand-green` | `#99C221` | 382 U · 153, 194, 33 | Logo "Ed" + D-mark, primary CTAs, eyebrows |
| `--brand-green-deep` | `#7DA019` | — | Hover / pressed |
| `--brand-green-soft` | `#DAE99F` | — | Soft pill fills, dividers |
| `--brand-green-tint` | `#F1F7DC` | — | Section / card tint |
| `--brand-blue` | `#454F7E` | 2757 U · 69, 79, 126 | Logo "MeCa", H3/H4, links, programme accents |
| `--brand-blue-deep` | `#353D5C` | — | Inverted card surfaces, pressed |
| `--brand-coral` | `#E15F55` | 179 U · 225, 95, 85 | **Accent only** — never large surfaces |
| `--brand-gray` / `--ink-20` | `#4C4E56` | Black 6 U · 76, 78, 86 | Primary text + logo gray (NOT pure black) |
| `--ink-95` | `#F7F8F5` | — | Cream page background |

Key rule from the brand guide: **only these four hues may colour the logomark**. Any other treatment requires brand-custodian approval.

### Type — per brand guide §6

- **Comfortaa Regular** — *logotype / wordmark only.* Don't use it for headings, body, or UI. The logo PNG already contains it.
- **Helvetica Neue Regular** — body copy, web, brochures, stationery, all correspondence.
  - **Web substitute:** **Inter** (free, near-twin grotesque). Flagged in *Caveats* — swap to licensed Helvetica Neue when the deliverable's licensing allows.
- **Eyebrows** (uppercase, 12 px, bold, `0.14em` tracking) are set in the same family as body — Inter / Helvetica Neue. Colour them **brand-green- deep** so they read against cream and white surfaces.

### Backgrounds

- **Cream (`#F7F8F5`) is the default canvas.** Pure white is reserved for cards / surfaces that need to lift.
- **Sage tint (`#F1F7DC`)** is the section accent — used full-bleed for WHO-WE-ARE / OUR-PROGRAMMES blocks on the one-pager.
- **No photography, no full-bleed imagery** in the source material. The brand is type-and-shape forward. If a layout needs a hero, prefer a flat composition of programme icons or a sage-tint colour block over a stock photo of business people.
- **No gradients, ever.** Flat fills only.

### Layout & spacing

- **4-px base spacing scale** (`--s-1 … --s-24`).
- **Generous gutters between sections** (`--s-12` and up) — the one-pager breathes; copy is never wall-to-wall.
- **Grid-and-card layout.** Sections sit in cream space; content lives in white cards with thin warm-grey borders. Programmes group as 3-column card sets.
- **Fixed elements** are minimal — header + footer. Avoid sticky overlays unless functionally required.

### Borders, corners, elevation

- **Corner radii:** 8 px (cards), 4 px (chips, small fields), 999 px (pills & tier badges). 12–16 px only on hero / programme cards. No fully-square hard corners; no super-rounded "pillow" cards.
- **Borders are visible.** A 1 px warm-grey hairline (`--ink-80`) on every card; that hairline IS the design — shadows are secondary.
- **Shadows are soft and paper-like.** A 1–2 px ambient + a 6–8 px diffuse, both at very low opacity (≤8%). Never coloured shadows, never blurs above 24 px.
- **No inner shadows.** No glassmorphism / no backdrop-filter (except an optional 8 px backdrop on a sticky header — see Header.jsx).

### Imagery & illustration

- **No photography is established yet.** When introducing photography, prefer warm, naturally-lit, candid scenes of workshops, whiteboards, facilitators with participants. Avoid corporate stock. Light grain is fine; heavy filters are not.
- **Illustration style** (when needed): line-and-fill, two-tone, using brand green + brand blue + white, matching the D-mark's whiteboard-and-video-bubble construction. Stroke weights consistent at roughly 6–8 % of icon height.
- **Coral is for accent only** — never as a flat background fill larger than a chip / dot / underline.
- **No hand-drawn / sketchy textures.** No repeating patterns. No grain overlays on solid blocks.

### Animation & motion

- **Restrained.** This is a B2B / education brand; motion should feel considered, not playful.
- **Durations:** 120 / 200 / 320 ms. Anything over 400 ms reads sluggish.
- **Easing:** mostly `--ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Reserve `--ease-standard` for state changes.
- **Hovers** are a 2–3 % darkening of fill (`--brand-green-deep` for green buttons; never a brightness filter) plus a 1–2 px elevation lift on cards. No translateY past 2 px.
- **Press** states are a 1 % inset darken — no scale.
- **No bounces, no springy overshoots.** No infinite-loop "shimmer" or pulse states; loaders are linear progress bars or a single 600 ms fade.

### Transparency & blur

- **Almost none.** Surfaces are opaque. Use `rgba()` only for hairline hover rings (`--shadow-ring`) and for low-opacity neutrals on disabled states. A 8 px sticky-header backdrop is the only acceptable blur.

### Cards (the recurring object)

A typical EdMeCa card is:

- White surface
- 1 px `--ink-80` hairline
- 12–16 px radius
- `--shadow-1` ambient (lifts on hover to `--shadow-2`)
- 24–32 px internal padding
- A short eyebrow at top (green-deep, uppercase), an `<h3>` in brand-blue, one paragraph in ink-40, optional bottom-row metadata.

## Iconography

The brand guide is **silent on iconography** — it covers logomark, colour, and typography only. The one-pager carries no decorative icons. So there is no proprietary icon set to lift.

**Substitution.** We use **[Lucide](https://lucide.dev)** (via CDN, `unpkg.com/lucide@latest`) as the default icon set:

- Line-style, 1.75 – 2 px stroke, square caps.
- Matches the construction of the logo's whiteboard mark.
- Comprehensive (workshop / education / business glyphs available: `presentation`, `target`, `book-open`, `lightbulb`, `users`, `award`, `bar-chart`, `compass`, `route`).

🚩 **Flagged substitution.** Treat Lucide as a placeholder until EdMeCa delivers its own icon set.

**Rules of use**

- Stroke icons at 1.75–2 px, never filled by default.
- Colour: **`--brand-blue`** (`#454F7E`) on cream/white; white on dark surfaces. Coral is reserved for status/alert icons.
- Two-tone only for hero illustrations (green stroke + blue accent), never in inline body copy.
- **No emoji** anywhere in product copy or marketing surfaces.
- **No unicode symbol-as-icon** (✓ / ★ / → are OK *as typographic punctuation* but never as a UI affordance — use the corresponding Lucide icon).
- **Sizes:** 16 px (inline), 20 px (buttons), 24 px (card headers), 32–48 px (programme cards / pillars), 64–96 px (hero illustrations).

## Logo rules (recap from brand guide)

- **Clear space** = 50 % of the logo's height on each side, measured by the width of the "D" creative shape (brand guide §2).
- **Variations allowed:** Full colour · One colour · Full colour with background · One colour reversed (§3).
- **Don't** resize parts independently, don't substitute the font, don't recolour the mark outside the four primary colours.
- **Don't** stretch, compress, or rotate.

The logo PNGs in `assets/` are the only approved bitmaps in this system. Use them as-is; never re-render the wordmark from Comfortaa unless you have brand-custodian sign-off.

## CAVEATS

1. **Helvetica Neue is paid-licence.** We've subbed **Inter** for web work. Use the licensed Helvetica Neue in print / native apps where it's available.
2. **No live product to mirror.** Without codebase or Figma access, the `ui_kits/website/` kit is a *plausible* marketing site styled to the brand guide + one-pager — not a recreation of edmeca.co.za. Please share the live site, a Figma, or screenshots so we can tighten this kit.
3. **Iconography is placeholder (Lucide).** The brand guide doesn't prescribe an icon set; we picked one. Replace if EdMeCa standardises.
4. **Imagery rules are extrapolated.** The brand guide is silent on photography and illustration. The guidance under *Imagery & illustration* is a recommendation, not lifted from official material.
5. **No tone / casing rules document existed.** Voice guidance is reverse- engineered from the one-pager only.
6. **Colour-derived scales (`-soft`, `-tint`, `-deep`) are computed**, not taken from the brand guide. They're tonal lightnesses of the canonical Pantone hex; trustworthy for digital, but check before print.

**Please help us tighten this by sharing:** the live website, a Figma if one exists, the licensed Helvetica Neue files, photography / illustration examples (if any), and 2–3 long-form copy samples (blog, programme proposal, partner deck).

### What was translated, and what was refused

- The reference deck's warm orange, black and yellow ramp became the EdMeCa ramp: blue-deep, blue, green-deep, green. Coral stays an accent (bullets, one word in a title), never a surface.
- Its condensed display face became Inter 800 uppercase with 0.02 em tracking. Its script accents became a light-weight tracked word in white.
- Its paper texture and paper-plane corner device were not carried over: the brand has no textures (Visual foundations) and the corner device is the D-mark.
- Its full-colour continent map was not carried over: the brand has no approved map asset, so the footprint slide uses callouts.

### Rules of use

- Titles are uppercase, 40 px, Inter 800, blue on cream and white on dark; one word may be green.
- Dark slides use `--brand-blue-deep`, never black.
- Footer left is a plain note; footer right is the brand line in green.
- No em dashes and no section symbols in slide copy, in line with EdMeCa document conventions (the earlier Mechanics note on em dashes is superseded for all documents, spreadsheets and presentations).
- Naming in copy: EdMeCa. Edmeca (Pty) Ltd is the registered company and trades as EdMeCa; write the legal name only where a document needs it (contracts, invoices, the footer of a formal brief), as "Edmeca (Pty) Ltd, trading as EdMeCa". Never EDMECA or Edmeca in running text.

## Contrast rule for text on brand green (adopted 3 November 2026)

Measured against WCAG AA: brand green on white is 2.1:1 and green-deep on cream is 2.9:1, so neither may carry small text. Ink-10 on green is 7.7:1, white on blue 7.9:1, white on blue-deep 10.6:1, green on blue-deep 5.1:1.

- Primary buttons: green fill with ink-10 text, never white text on green. Secondary buttons: blue outline.
- Eyebrows: brand blue on cream and white surfaces; brand green on blue-deep surfaces. This supersedes the "colour them brand-green-deep" line under Type for light surfaces.
- Green remains free for fills, rules, dots, bullets, pill backgrounds and one word in a heading of 24 px or more on blue-deep.
