## 1. Strategy on a page

### Value statement (adopted 31 October 2026)

Long form, once per page:

EdMeCa turns learning into earning.

Our research-backed, AI-enabled programmes make young people work-ready, help entrepreneurs build businesses that last, and equip leaders to grow capability across their teams. We measure what changes and share it.

Short form, for footers and sign-offs: Research-backed AI capability that turns learning into earning.

Rules: keep the three beneficiary groups in that order (young people, entrepreneurs, leaders) and keep the measurement sentence.

### Our Values (confirmed 3 November 2026, exact wording)

- Empowerment: We uplift and enable youth, entrepreneurs and business leaders to reach their full potential.
- Collaboration: We build an ecosystem where people, ideas and opportunities connect.
- Impact: We measure our success by the positive, lasting change we create in society.

### Design principles (internal; not shown on the site)

QCTO alignment for all new content; corporates and ESD first; never free; one playbook; no single-funder dependency.

### End game (internal; not shown on the site)

The ten-year ambition is not named publicly. The external wording is "accredited pathways". Do not mention a university.

### Brand position

EdMeCa builds its own content and delivers under its own brand, co-branded with a programme owner where that helps. The portal is the AI Business Studio, offered as post-programme access, not a subscription. The learning platform name "Online Academy" is retired.

## 2. Customer Segments

### Who benefits (in this order)

1. Young people: made work-ready; Youthpreneur; accredited pathways.
2. Entrepreneurs and SMEs: businesses that last; a usable output from every session.
3. Leaders in corporates and ecosystems: Digital Leadership, meaning leading, and digital.

### Who commissions programmes (the payers)

- Corporates and skills development foundations, buying for their executives and management teams.
- ESD and supplier development programme owners (the corporate funding the programme, or an intermediary), buying the AI and digital module inside their SME cohorts plus a before-and-after impact report.
- Learning institutions and TVET colleges, buying modules for their incubators and students, and staff capacitation.
- SETAs and training authorities, through accredited skills programmes.
- Funders and foundations, paying for NGO work and for scale.

### Who we do not serve

NGOs without a named funder; individuals paying out of pocket; cold outbound. Subscriptions for entrepreneurs are ruled out.

### Priority for the next 6 to 18 months

Corporates and ESD first; institutions next; NGOs only when a funder is named; grants built in parallel.

## 3. Value Propositions

### Opening line

EdMeCa develops people to become future tech leaders, whether they are young people, entrepreneurs or leaders inside organisations and ecosystems. We do it by delivering proven frameworks through AI-enabled, practical digital tools, with evidence of the shift. From framework to execution to evidence.

### Our proposition

A unique blend of industry insight, academic rigour and practical digital tools, delivered by facilitators working from one playbook. We develop people to become future tech leaders. We measure the shift and share the evidence.

### Digital Leadership as the positioning

Digital Leadership means leading, and digital. Leaders do not need to be digital experts, but they need enough insight to lead where digital, AI and cyber risk shape the business, together with established leadership competencies. Cyber security sits inside Digital Leadership. This is the natural overlap we position on, for corporates, ecosystems and institutions alike.

### Market offering stack

| Offering | What it is | Primary segments |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital Leader Module | Signature opener. 10-dimension baseline, maturity scale 1 to 4, cohort profiling, AI opportunity map. The PRE in PRE and POST. | All |
| AI for Leaders and the AI Catalyst Journey | Executive session, then a six-session programme for teams. | Corporates, institutions |
| AI and digital module inside an SMME cohort | Delivered under the EdMeCa brand, co-branded with the programme owner where that helps, with impact report. | ESD programme owners |
| Impact Reporting | Standalone service: baseline, mid, post, consolidated report within 10 business days, formatted for funder reporting. The entry product. | ESD, funders, institutions |

### What we claim, and what we do not

We claim what the evidence pack shows: measurable maturity shift per participant, tools adopted, a usable business output from every session, and funder-ready evidence. We do not claim revenue multiples. Where a client needs financial growth and job creation reported, we define the measure, the timeframe and the attribution method before the programme starts.

Website rule (3 November 2026): the evidence section carries these four claims only. No figures and no case tiles until Raymond supplies them. Evidence, when added, is described by programme type (an enterprise and supplier development cohort; an entrepreneur bootcamp run with an accelerator), never by client name.

### Language rules

Use: AI and digital; practical digital tools; future tech leaders; research-backed; from framework to execution to evidence.
Do not use: AI and technology; AI tools (for the toolkit); 10X; first in the world; customers switch from generic providers; white-label; unlimited; free account.

### How we work (the January 2026 lesson)

AI onboarding first; then frameworks and a tools refresh; then a sprint to a usable output; then measure and report (PRE, MID, POST).

## 4. Channels

### Ranked channels

1. Existing clients (run now).
2. ESD and programme partners (run now).
3. Corporate and TVET supplier databases, using Level One B-BBEE (next).
4. Workshops at events (next).
5. LinkedIn and case studies (next).
6. Funders and foundations (build).

Not for us yet: cold email and paid advertising.

### Sales process

Warm introduction; discovery call; proposal within five working days; paid first session; expand and ask for a referral. Never free (the free diagnostics are the baseline instrument, not a free session).

### Two routes

Relationship route: MOU, then SLA, then revenue. Procurement route: RFQ, RFT or EOI, then award; supplier database registration is the prerequisite.

### Website implications

Primary CTA everywhere: "Book a discovery call". Secondary: "Take the free baseline" (the diagnostic). Contact page promises a response within one business day and a proposal within five working days of a discovery call.

## 5. Revenue Streams and pricing

Mechanisms: direct programme and session fees; Impact Reporting as a standalone service; grant funding through a PBO vehicle; partner resale on a negotiated share of net margin; accredited skills programmes through skills development budgets.

Pricing: built from a component catalogue and a rate card by client type; discounts stated upfront; business development engagements flagged separately. The website shows no prices.

Engagement tiers (from the January briefing, recorded in the design system):

- Focused Session: a single targeted intervention, for example a BMC workshop, a design sprint or pitch prep.
- Mid-Tier: 4 to 5 targeted interventions, scoped outcomes, measurable participant progression.
- Full Journey: a 12-month partnership with comprehensive tooling, multiple touchpoints, and baseline-to-outcome impact reporting.
